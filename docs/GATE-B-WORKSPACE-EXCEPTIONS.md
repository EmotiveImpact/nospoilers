## Implementation contract

1. Give exceptions immutable workspace ownership and stable internal actor identities. Bind a request to a specific source or artifact scope, rule and path; never default silently to all workspace assets. Validate justification and future expiry using existing policy validation with bounded input lengths.
2. Store requested, approved, rejected and revoked state with append-only events. Expiry is evaluated at use time, not dependent on a background process. Requests must be idempotent; conflicting approval/revocation must be serialized and audited in the same transaction.
3. Separate request authority from approval authority. Enforce live workspace role, membership, archive and subscription checks server-side. When independent approval is required, the request author cannot approve, including after a rename or role change. A missing eligible approver is a visible blocked workflow, not permission to self-approve.
4. Capture only currently approved, unexpired, correctly scoped exceptions when a new scan starts. Reuse `applyPolicy` and its suppressed-finding evidence. Later approval, expiry or revocation must never rewrite an old report, receipt, digest or verdict, and must not automatically resolve an exposure alert.
5. Preserve connected exception behavior and history while migrating ownership; do not copy an installation's exceptions into an unrelated workspace. Mixed connected/independent policy selection needs explicit source scope and acceptance tests.
6. Present requests and their status in workspace policy settings, with a contextual entry from the selected finding. Explain that accepting risk is not fixing exposure. Show requester, approver, justification, rule/path/source scope, expiry and audit history. Release detail shows the exception snapshot actually used, not the current mutable configuration.

## Acceptance evidence required

- Own versus foreign workspace/source/attempt tests, viewer and revoked-member denial, archive and expired-plan behavior.
- Request deduplication, simultaneous decision tests, self-approval denial when required, approver revocation, expiry and immutable scope/events.
- A real scan with one approved matching exception and one unrelated finding; pending/rejected/revoked/expired exceptions do not suppress anything.
- Old report/receipt remain byte-for-byte unchanged after an exception decision or expiry; no scan-job side effects from reading or approving an exception.
- Connected legacy upgrade and mixed-source fixtures retain identity, history and scope.
- Rendered request/review/revoke/error states, deep links and refresh/back navigation; correct disabled-action explanations.

SSO, SCIM and enterprise custom-role implementation remain separate gates. Do not introduce simulated approvals or label unimplemented policy controls as active.
