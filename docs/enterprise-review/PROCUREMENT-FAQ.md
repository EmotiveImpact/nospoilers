# Procurement FAQ

**Review draft, not binding terms or an approved enterprise offer.**

## What is being evaluated?

A release-exposure product with authenticated archive checks, supported source monitoring, retained results, response workflows and scoped proof. Confirm the actual input types, workspace roles, provider configuration and operational status for the buyer. Implementation evidence is not a hosted-service guarantee.

## What commercial information is confirmed?

The public code and approved product direction specify a five-day trial, Solo USD 29 monthly / USD 290 annually and Team USD 99 monthly / USD 990 annually. There is no permanent free scanner. Annual amounts are ten monthly payments for a year, not “ten months for the price of twelve”. Confirm the configured price IDs, checkout total and applicable tax treatment before contracting.

Enterprise price, invoice terms, purchase-order acceptance, included seats, workspace caps, discounts and minimum commitments are not established by this draft. Do not infer them from provisional defaults or an enabled UI control.

## Is a GitHub installation required for every use case?

No. Independent workspace archive/website paths exist. GitHub source access still requires an authorised App installation; product sign-in is currently GitHub-based. Do not confuse independent source ownership with already implemented email/Google/Microsoft sign-in or SSO.

## Can the buyer use SSO, SCIM or dedicated infrastructure?

These are not operational features promised by the current offer. A buyer with mandatory enterprise identity, private networking, residency or dedicated-worker requirements must have them explicitly reviewed before the pilot proceeds. Do not sell a disabled or planned control as available.

## What is included in the evaluation?

Only the scope agreed in a pilot plan. Use [proposed measurable criteria](PILOT-EVALUATION-PLAN.md) to agree inputs, evidence, test identities and go/no-go. No fixed duration, custom implementation, unlimited usage or response-time guarantee is implied.

## How are support and security questions raised?

The existing repository contact is `emotiveimpact@gmail.com`. Public support and enterprise pages expose email links, not a submission backend. Confirm the buyer's required support channel, hours and response targets separately. Do not represent this as an emergency response desk.

## Can procurement receive an approved DPA or security attestation?

This repository does not establish that an approved DPA, certification, penetration-test report or security attestation is available. The [security questionnaire](SECURITY-QUESTIONNAIRE.md) is a review draft and must be checked against live operational evidence. Existing website policy text remains visibly unapproved draft material until review.

## What happens when access or service ends?

Disconnection stops monitoring but retains authorised evidence. Subscription cancellation is a billing action, not history-deletion consent. Archiving preserves history and restricts new work. A deletion review request is not a completed purge or account closure. Exact evidence visibility, retention obligations, backup treatment and any legal holds need review; do not promise universal immediate erasure.

## What needs approval before a paid engagement?

Confirm the legal contracting entity and authorised signatory, current hosted readiness, scope and price, tax/invoice arrangements, approved terms/privacy/DPA, subprocessors/region, support model and retention/termination conditions. Do not introduce invented customer references, testimonials, uptime claims or operational enterprise features to fill missing answers.
