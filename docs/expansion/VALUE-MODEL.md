# NoSpoilers value model

This is a scenario model, not a promise or fundraising valuation.

## Assumptions

- Locked prices: Solo $29/month, Team $99/month.
- Customer mix: 85% Solo, 15% Team.
- Blended monthly recurring revenue per account: approximately $39.50.
- Figures below are gross recurring revenue before Stripe, hosting, support, tax and refunds.
- Valuation ranges use conservative 2026 private/micro-SaaS outcomes, not public cybersecurity
  company multiples.

## Indicative business value

Code and PRDs without users/revenue have uncertain resale value. Buyers pay for recurring revenue,
retention, distribution, operating independence and verified security—not feature count.

For a small bootstrapped product, use roughly 2.5–4× ARR as an ordinary range and 4–6.5× only when
growth, retention, margin and founder independence are strong.

Cybersecurity can earn higher strategic multiples, but typically only with meaningful scale,
30%+ growth, strong net revenue retention, low customer concentration, and enterprise-quality
operations. Do not value an early product at 10–15× based on category headlines.

## What increases value

- Gross logo churn below 3% monthly at small-business scale.
- Net revenue retention above 100%.
- Automatic npm/release/web coverage embedded in customer workflow.
- High percentage of releases scanned without manual action.
- Low false-positive and notification-failure rates.
- More Team mix without services-heavy support.
- Gross margin above 75–80%.
- No customer representing more than 10–15% of revenue.
- Documented operations, tests, incident response and low founder dependency.
- Defensible historical artifact manifests/policies without retaining source.

## What destroys value

- Revenue depending on one disclosure campaign or customer.
- High churn after the first incident is fixed.
- Free CLI satisfying the full job without hosted value.
- Unbounded scan compute or poor gross margin.
- False credential accusations.
- Storing customer source/secrets.
- Platform-policy violations or excessive GitHub permissions.
- Multiple half-built products sharing one database and support burden.

## Portfolio value

The four standalone PRDs have no financial value merely because they are written or built. Each
must independently earn recurring revenue. Do not add hypothetical valuations together.

The portfolio becomes strategically useful if:

- NoSpoilers owns release confidentiality.
- Employee Public Footprint owns employee-origin exposure.
- Release Ledger owns artifact integrity.
- Package Impersonation Monitor owns package identity.
- Disclosure Desk owns the human coordination workflow.

Shared distribution and trust can reduce acquisition cost, but production data and permissions
remain isolated.

## Practical target

NoSpoilers becomes a meaningful small business around 300–500 paying accounts:

- $11.9k–$19.8k MRR
- $142k–$237k ARR
- Indicative ordinary value around $425k–$950k
- Potentially above $1M with strong retention, growth and operating independence

The ultimate feature set raises the chance of retention and Team adoption. It does not create value
until customers use and renew it.
