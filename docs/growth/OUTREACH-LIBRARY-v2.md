# Outreach library v2: evidence-led and billing-aware

8 September 2026. Supersedes the earlier conversation's overbroad release-assurance claims. These are manual copy templates, not an implemented sending system. Complete placeholders and confirm the current live capability before sending. No recipients were contacted in this work.

## Operator rules

Describe what the product actually supports and what this host has operationally verified. Do not sell the roadmap, source-map detection as universal security, a perpetual archive, agency portfolio management or an autonomous agent as already live.

The current plans are Solo $29/month and Team $99/month, USD, with the shared five-day trial. Do not promise a longer/no-card/no-charge evaluation or renewal behaviour unless specifically approved and correctly provisioned. The original “paid pilot” phrase is not the default. NoSpoilers does not pay companies to evaluate it. A special assisted free pilot is an owner-approved exception, not a currently implemented entitlement.

Stop a sales sequence on reply, opt-out or a routing request; use at most two relevant follow-ups. Check applicable marketing/privacy requirements and the suppression list. Do not manufacture scarcity, customer names, user counts, incidents or benefits. Add your real sender details and a clear way to decline further contact. Security reporting uses a separate approved channel and never includes a purchase requirement.
