What Linear is doing — chrome lighting, not a grey page

On linear.app the product shot is a darker frame around a darker canvas. The lighter grey in the top-left is a radial luminance wash (their edgeGlow is white at about 4–8% over #090a0b / #101112, which reads as ~#161618) plus a 1px edge highlight / shine masked to that same corner. The sidebar also gets a small blurred bloom behind the wordmark. The inner issue view stays near-black. A vignette under the window makes the shell feel heavy.

These six directions put that idea on the live Watch desk — rail #050506, stage #09090b, 16px inset, Alerts C (30px title + four pills). Click A–F to retarget the same desk. Live Watch is unchanged until one is picked.

Not this: filling Overview or Alerts with mid-grey (#141416). This: light on the rail, gutter, top edge, or command strip.
